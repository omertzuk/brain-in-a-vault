Hello! This is the [[Brain in a Vault]] of [[Omer Tzuk]].

I'm a physicist and complex-systems researcher turned consciousness scientist. My background is in astrophysics and cosmology — B.Sc. at the University of Bologna, M.Sc. in Gravitation and Astroparticle Physics at the University of Amsterdam — followed by a Ph.D. in the physics of complex systems at Ben-Gurion University, where I studied pattern formation and self-organization in dryland ecosystems. I am currently a postdoctoral fellow at the [Contemplative Neuroscience and Neurophenomenology Lab](https://avivabo.edu.haifa.ac.il/) at the University of Haifa, where I study EEG, predictive processing, and altered states of consciousness.

This digital garden is my thinking space — a place where I work through ideas rather than present finished ones. It grows alongside my research, and its structure reflects the way I actually think: concepts linking to concepts, questions opening into more questions.

The central preoccupation here is **how minds model the world** — and how those models can shift. The threads I follow most closely:

- [[Predictive Processing]] and the [[Bayesian Brain]] hypothesis
- [[Active Inference]] and the [[Free Energy Principle]]
- [[Neurophenomenology]] and first-person methods in consciousness science
- [[4E Cognition]] — embodied, embedded, enacted, extended mind
- [[Information Theory]] as a cross-domain language for complexity
- [[Altered States of Consciousness]] and belief revision

If you're arriving from outside, the [[Z to A/|Concepts]] page is a good place to orient. My full profile is at [omertzuk.github.io](https://omertzuk.github.io/).
